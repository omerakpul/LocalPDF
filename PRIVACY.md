# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-12_

LocalPDF is an offline PDF toolbox. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## In short

* LocalPDF **does not collect, store on a server, or share** any of your data.
* The app **does not request the internet permission**, so it cannot upload your files, even by accident.
* There are **no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting services** in the app.

## What stays on your device

All work happens locally:

* Documents you pick (PDF, Word, images) are read only to perform the operation you asked for. Temporary copies are created in the app's cache and deleted afterwards.
* Results are saved in the app's own folder on your device. If you choose **Save to Downloads**, a copy is placed in your Downloads folder; **PDF → Images** saves images to `Pictures/LocalPDF`.
* A small local database stores the names, paths, sizes and dates of files you saved, so the Files and Recent Files lists work. Your settings (theme, language) are stored in the app's preferences.
* Passwords you type for protecting or unlocking a PDF are used only for that operation and are not stored.

## Permissions

* **Storage (Android 12 and lower only):** used to save files to Downloads/Pictures on older Android versions. On newer versions the app uses the system file picker and does not need broad storage access.

## Sharing

The app only shares a file when you press **Share** or open a file with another app. The file is then handed to the app you select through Android's share sheet; what that app does with it is covered by its own privacy policy.

## Links

The Settings screen has a link to this project's GitHub page. It opens in your browser and is subject to GitHub's privacy policy.

## Backups

Android cloud backup does not include your generated PDFs or the file list. Settings may be included in the standard Android backup.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository with an updated date.

## Contact

Questions or concerns: please open an issue at https://github.com/omerakpul/LocalPDF/issues.
